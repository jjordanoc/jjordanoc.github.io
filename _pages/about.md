I'm Joaquin, an MS in Computer Science student at [Columbia University](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/) (graduating December 2027), where I'm a graduate student researcher at the Mobile X Lab. I'm at Columbia on a full-tuition scholarship, which I received after graduating first in my class from [UTEC](https://utec.edu.pe/) in Lima, Peru. I also spent a semester at UC Berkeley on a UTEC scholarship, doing research on AI and education. At 19, I co-founded Syntax, a speech-to-speech personalized AI English tutor for upskilling professionals in Latin America.

**I'm looking for Summer 2027 internships in Applied Science and ML Engineering.** Reach out at [j.jordan@columbia.edu](mailto:j.jordan@columbia.edu)!

## 🔭 What I'm working on

- **BlinkGS**: 3D Gaussian Splatting under varying illumination and low-light conditions. _Mobile X Lab, Columbia_ · [GitHub](https://github.com/Rennylex/BlinkGS)
- **CA-Gate**: flexible conditional activation steering using gates. · [GitHub](https://github.com/tklapp/steering_repo)

## 🗂️ What I've worked on

- **M3aSURE** (under review): probing 3D transformer latent spaces for geometric consistency. A reference-free metric that reaches 0.874 AUC vs. a 0.796 baseline. _UTEC AI Research Group, 2025–26_ · [GitHub](https://github.com/jjordanoc/m3asure)
- **mmWave 3D pose**: 3D pose reconstruction with privacy-preserving mmWave sensing, used to steer a ground drone with pointing gestures. _TU Delft Networked Systems Group, 2026_ · [GitHub](https://github.com/barrydoooit/mwpose3d) <br> <small>3D Vision · 3D Sensing · Deep Learning · 3D Pose Estimation</small>
- **MAGIC** (EAAI/AAAI-26): a multi-agent evaluation system for education. Five LLM agents plus an orchestrator raised agreement with human essay scores from 0.613 to 0.766 QWK. _UC Berkeley, 2025_ · [GitHub](https://github.com/magic-aes/MAGIC) <br> <small>Modal · vLLM · HuggingFace · LLM fine-tuning · LLM evaluation</small>
- **Syntax** (Co-founder & CTO): speech-to-speech personalized AI English tutor; grew to 5,000+ users and supported a \$125k pre-seed raise. _Lima, 2023–24_
