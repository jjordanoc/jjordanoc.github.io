**Role:** Visiting Undergraduate Researcher, AI and Education — University of California, Berkeley (Jan 2025 – May 2025)

**Stack:** Modal · vLLM · HuggingFace · LLM fine-tuning · LLM evaluation

- Proposed and implemented a zero-shot framework with 5 specialized LLM agents and an orchestrator, improving agreement with human essay scores from **0.613** to **0.766 QWK** over a single-agent baseline.
- Designed and ran experiments across 3 open-weight LLMs, building the vLLM inference and evaluation stack for scoring, per-trait reliability, and LLM-as-a-judge feedback evaluation.
